Review Guidelines to Qualify for Your Reward

Following these guidelines makes sure your review qualifies and your reward is processed without delays.

Contact us before leaving less than 5 stars

If something went wrong, reach out first. You can still earn your reward. We just ask for a chance to help before you publish a lower rating.

Write a meaningful title

Use a specific title like “Celoxis simplified our entire delivery workflow” rather than a generic “Review of Celoxis.” Good titles help other buyers find what they need.

Select both categories

On G2 and Capterra, choose both the “Project Management” and “Project Portfolio Management” categories. Also rate Ease of Use, Meets Requirements, and Quality of Support.

Do not post anonymously

Your name must be visible in the review so we can verify it’s you and process your gift card. Anonymous reviews do not qualify for rewards.

Avoid grammar and spelling mistakes

A well-written review carries more credibility. If you’d like help drafting your review, reach out and we’ll be happy to assist.

Skip the word “trial”

If you’re on a trial and love Celoxis, you’re welcome to write a review. Just avoid mentioning “trial” in your review text.

Have a concern? Contact us first

Found something missing or something you didn’t like? Email us before writing about it. Celoxis often has features and workarounds that might surprise you.

Send a screenshot after publishing

Once your review is live, email a screenshot to marketing@celoxis.com. We’ll verify it meets our guidelines and process your reward within 3 to 5 business days.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get paid to write reviews online?

Write an honest review of Celoxis on G2, Capterra, or Gartner Peer Insights. Once it’s published, email a screenshot to marketing@celoxis.com. We verify that your review meets our guidelines and send your Amazon gift card within 3 to 5 business days.

How much can I earn writing reviews for Celoxis?

You can earn a $15 Amazon gift card for each published review on G2, Capterra, or Gartner Peer Insights.

How long does it take to write a review and earn money?

A G2 or Capterra review takes about 5 to 10 minutes. A Gartner Peer Insights review takes around 15 minutes. Once your review is published and verified, your reward is sent within 3 to 5 business days.

Can I get paid to write reviews if I’m from a different country?

Yes. The Review & Earn program is open to Celoxis users worldwide. Amazon gift cards work in any country where Amazon is available. If your local currency differs from USD or INR, we send the equivalent amount in your local currency.

Can I still earn if I leave less than 5 stars?

Yes. You can still earn your reward with a review below 5 stars. The only requirement is that you contact us at marketing@celoxis.com before publishing. Tell us what went wrong and we’ll try to explain, offer a workaround, or resolve the issue.

Do I need to be a paid customer to write a review and earn?

You should be a genuine user of Celoxis. If you’re on a trial and love the product, you’re welcome to write a review. All major review platforms require honest reviews from real users.

How long does it take to receive my Amazon gift card?

After we receive your screenshot and verify the review, we process your reward within 3 business days for G2 and Capterra, or 5 business days for Gartner Peer Insights.

Which platform gives the highest reward?

All three platforms offer the same $15 Amazon gift card. If you want the quickest option, G2 takes about 5 minutes. If you’re an enterprise or IT user, Gartner Peer Insights is a good fit.
