QUESTIONS on Claap G2 reviews

Can’t find the answer here? Contact us

How long does it take for me to get my voucher?

Once you’ve submitted your G2 review, keep an eye out for a notification saying your review has been accepted. Keep in mind it can take up to three business days for the G2 team to accept your review. Take a screenshot, fill in this form, and we’ll send you your voucher typically within 2-3 weeks.

Do I have to leave a good G2 review?

The short answer is no. Leave an honest review that reflects your experience with Claap. On the other hand, if you really want to help us out, a good review would do so much. It’s competitive out there and even having a score under 5/5 is enough to put people off these days. We are working hard to make Claap customers happy, hope you are too!

How do I get a G2 account?

You can sign up at g2.com/signup. You can even log in with your LinkedIn account to save time. You’ll just need to send the G2 team some proof you are a Claap customer, usually with a screenshot of your interface, to confirm you’re eligible to write a review.

What happens if my review is rejected?

If your review is rejected, you will get a rejection email letting you know the rejection reason. We sadly can’t send any vouchers for rejected reviews. You can also login to your G2 account and navigate to the Reviews section to learn more about why the review was not accepted.
