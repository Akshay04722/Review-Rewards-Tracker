FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ’s)

How long does it take to receive my reward?

It can take up to three business days for each review to be certified by Capterra, Revver, and G2. Please use your work email address when submitting your review. Revver uses this information to confirm customer eligibility before issuing rewards. Once your review has been verivied, your reward will be sent the next business day to the email address used during submission.

Can I submit more than one review of Revver on each site?

We encourage you to leave one review of Revver on each site. You personally cannot submit more than one review on Capterra or G2, but multiple people at your company can submit a review of Revver and receive a reward. Each reviewer will need to have a distinct email address. 

How long will this reward program last?

There is no end date for either reward program at this time. If you click the links and they are active, you can rest assured that you will receive a $25 gift card for completing a certified review. 

Will I receive a reward if I leave a review without using the links above?

No. If you go to Revver’s profile on Capterra or G2 and click the Submit Review button, you won’t be eligible for a reward. The links above are unique to Revver’s incentive program. Be sure to click on them. They will send you to the proper page that lets you get a $25 gift card at the end.
