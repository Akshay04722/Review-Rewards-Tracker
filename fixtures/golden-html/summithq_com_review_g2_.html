How it works

Four steps, and most of the work is on G2’s side.

The fine print

$50 USD or local equivalent. Your review has to be of a Summit service you currently use, it has to be submitted through one of the links on this page, and it has to be approved by G2's QA team to qualify. Approved reviewers get an email from G2 within three business days with a Reward Link, which lets you choose from a catalog of cards available in your country of residence, Amazon included. Preview the catalog.

The redemption link is provided on a promotional basis and is not a gift card or prepaid card. It has to be redeemed within a year of being received, after which it expires and can't be used to claim a reward. No fees apply before expiration. Once redeemed, the card you choose is subject to the terms of that retailer or provider. G2 sets the review standards and handles moderation, so Summit can't influence whether a review is accepted or what it says. G2's terms of use and Reward Link's terms of service apply.
